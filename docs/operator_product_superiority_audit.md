## 3. The 5 Core Operator Design Test Answers

1. **"Can a new user understand what the autonomous system is doing within 10 seconds?"**
   - **YES**. The Command Center and Mission Cockpit immediately show:
     - The mission objective (e.g. *"Enterprise Customer Migration & Sharding"*).
     - The active DAG stage and active frontier node.
     - Cline's primary brain state (*"EXECUTING"* / *"THINKING"*).
2. **"Can they determine whether Cline needs them within 5 seconds?"**
   - **YES**. The persistent top bar badge and **"NEEDS YOU" Action Center** prominently pulse in high-contrast amber with direct 1-click review triggers.
3. **"Can they inspect why an action happened?"**
   - **YES**. Clicking any DAG node opens the **Node Inspector**, revealing the tool arguments, execution rationale, responsible agent, and SHA-256 evidence proof.
4. **"Can they intervene safely?"**
   - **YES**. Dedicated, fail-safe controls allow 1-click **Approve**, **Reject**, **Pause Frontier**, **Resume**, or global **Emergency Kill-Switch**.
5. **"Can they prove what happened afterward?"**
   - **YES**. The **Evidence Explorer** provides an unbroken SHA-256 Merkle chain linking every tool call to immutable audit records with cryptographic lock badges.
